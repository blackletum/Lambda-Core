Lambda Core
Chapter 2 - Echoes From the Past

Eientei Dreamscape

First-person Perspective — Gameplay Mode

The screen slowly fades from darkness into reality over four seconds.

As Gordon Freeman's vision clears, he finds himself standing inside a confined chamber measuring approximately five by five meters with a ceiling height of two and a half meters.

A single bluish-purple light illuminates only the center of the room, leaving every corner hidden beneath darkness.

A solitary metal door stands directly ahead.

The room's architecture is identical to the Eientei corridors Gordon explored earlier. Yet something feels fundamentally different.

The walls...

The silence...

The atmosphere...

Everything suggests this place is no longer reality.

Gordon still carries his crowbar and shotgun.

He slowly approaches the door.

It slides open.

A narrow corridor extends twenty-five meters forward.

The ceiling height remains unchanged, while the hallway narrows to roughly two and a half meters wide.

Four dim ceiling lights cast the same bluish-purple glow seen in the previous room.

The facility is completely silent.

Only Gordon's footsteps echo softly through the corridor.

He reaches the end.

The hallway turns right.

Five meters ahead...

Another right turn.

Beyond it, another twenty-five-meter corridor stretches into darkness.

The lighting remains identical.

As Gordon continues forward, familiar details begin appearing across the walls.

A faded Lambda symbol.

Old Black Mesa facility markings.

Weathered identification stickers partially peeling from the concrete.

Fragments of a place he once knew.

Then...

A voice quietly echoes through the corridor.

Yuyuko Saigyouji:
"Gordon Freeman..."
「ゴードン・フリーマン……」

The voice disappears as suddenly as it arrived.

Silence returns.

Gordon continues walking.

The corridor bends right once again.

Another hallway stretches ten meters ahead.

A single door waits on the left side beneath another dim light.

Gordon opens it.

The room measures approximately eight by five meters.

Rows of desks and chairs line the left side.

A storage cabinet rests quietly in the far corner.

Unlike the corridors, this room is illuminated by a brighter ceiling light, allowing more of its surroundings to become visible.

Two doors stand before him.

One directly ahead.

Another on the right side.

The right-side door remains locked.

Gordon proceeds toward the door at the far end.

The next corridor extends to the right.

After ten meters...

The path turns right.

Then left.

Then right.

Before continuing straight for another five meters.

A small four-by-four-meter room waits at the end.

Beyond it...

Another corridor turns right.

The lighting throughout this section is noticeably brighter than before.

Gordon steps inside the room.

Without warning...

Two distorted zombies emerge from the darkness.

Gameplay begins.

The creatures rush toward Gordon.

After a brief firefight...

Both enemies collapse.

Instead of leaving behind bodies...

Their forms violently disintegrate into unstable energy, dissolving exactly like victims consumed by AR2 Combine energy balls.

Within seconds...

Nothing remains.

Only empty space.

The phenomenon silently reveals the truth.

These creatures are not ordinary zombies.

They are manifestations born from distorted youkai energy.

Silence returns.

The room itself is significantly darker than every area Gordon has explored so far.

He exits through the corridor on the right.

Seven meters ahead...

The hallway turns right.

Five meters later...

Left.

Another five meters...

A rapid left turn.

Immediately followed by a right turn.

Then another straight hallway measuring five meters.

At its end stands a reinforced sliding security door.

It remains locked.

Somewhere nearby...

Another mechanism controls it.

To Gordon's right...

A second doorway remains open.

He enters.

The room resembles an abandoned storage area.

Broken wooden furniture is scattered across the floor.

Tables.

Cabinets.

Chairs.

Everything appears randomly abandoned.

Three wooden crates sit near the center.

Gordon breaks them open.

Nothing.

Empty.

Rusted paint cans.

Glass jars.

Small debris litter the room.

Among every location in this level...

This room possesses the weakest lighting.

Darkness nearly consumes everything.

On the far-left side...

Another door quietly waits.

Gordon opens it.

The passage leads directly back into the previous eight-by-five-meter room.

A hidden shortcut.

Suddenly—

A Fast Zombie lunges from the darkness.

Gameplay resumes.

The creature rushes toward Gordon with terrifying speed.

Moments later...

It falls.

Just like the previous enemies...

Its body disintegrates into unstable AR2-like energy before completely disappearing.

Another distorted youkai manifestation...

Erased.

Silence returns once again.

On the right wall...

Gordon notices an unusual switch.

He presses it.

A distant mechanical sound echoes throughout the dreamscape.

Somewhere beyond the corridors...

The reinforced security door unlocks.

Beside the switch, a fully charged suit battery silently transfers its remaining energy into Gordon's HEV suit until the power reserve reaches maximum capacity.

Gordon returns to the locked security door.

As he approaches...

The motion sensor detects his presence.

The reinforced door slowly slides open.

Beyond it...

Another corridor extends five meters ahead.

The path turns right.

A staircase ascends at a gentle incline for approximately fifteen meters.

Gordon climbs upward in complete silence.

At the top...

A final corridor stretches another five meters.

A sliding door waits ahead.

As Gordon steps into the transition point located two meters before the door—

Loading...

No transition.
